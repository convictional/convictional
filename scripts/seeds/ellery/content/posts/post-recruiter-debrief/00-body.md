Quick thank-you to Leona Hart at Halverson Partners. The CoS funnel has been the best recruiter experience I have ever had as a founder — 60+ inbound, 12 screened, 3 real finalists in 18 days.

Three things she did that I want us to remember when we run the next senior search:
1. Aggressive screen on the scorecard instead of "would you be a fit for any role at Ellery"
2. Two-sided candidate debriefs — she calls the candidate after our loop and tells us what they thought
3. Transparent about who she is NOT sending us and why

Worth paying for when it is the right search.
