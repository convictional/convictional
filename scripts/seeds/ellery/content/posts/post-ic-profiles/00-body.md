The bar for senior ICs we hire in the next 12 months. Writing this down so our interview loops stay honest.

- **Owns a system end-to-end in production.** Not a feature. A system with on-call, dashboards, and a migration story.
- **Writes.** Design doc before code. If they cannot explain the trade-off in writing they cannot make it at scale.
- **Disagrees well.** Has a real opinion and loses it gracefully when the data changes.
- **Picks up the phone for customers.** The ones who will never ship legal-tech well are the ones who have never sat on a research call.

Applies to backend, ML, and full-stack alike. Scorecard updated.
