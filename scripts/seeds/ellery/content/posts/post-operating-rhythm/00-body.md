Draft operating rhythm for the exec team, now that we're 11 and climbing.

**Weekly:**
- Mon 10a: exec sync (60 min, decisions + blockers — not status)
- Wed 1p: deal review (30 min, Tessa drives; Darren joins when something needs unsticking)
- Fri 4p: async "what we decided this week" post (rotate authors)

**Bi-weekly:**
- Roadmap review (Leo drives, 90 min, every other Tuesday)
- Hiring review (whoever is closest to the loop drives, 30 min)

**Monthly:**
- All-hands (30 min)
- Exec 1:1s with Darren reset (45 min each)

**Quarterly:**
- Board meeting (day 1 of quarter)
- Exec offsite (day 2-3, offsite)
- Company OKRs reset

The point is to make space for the CoS once we have them — about half of this is stuff they'll own the mechanics on. Poke holes.
