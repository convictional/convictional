    <p style="font-family:Georgia,serif;font-size:18px;color:#1a1a2e;margin:0 0 12px 0;"><strong>What's working in enterprise legal-tech sales right now</strong></p>
    <p>The in-house legal market is buying differently in 2026. Budgets are tighter, buying committees are larger, and GCs are increasingly deferring to their legal ops directors on vendor selection. Three patterns from Pavilion members who closed enterprise legal-tech deals this quarter:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Security-first discovery.</strong> Reps who lead with "let me understand your data governance requirements" before the demo are converting discovery calls to demos at twice the rate of those who lead with product. The CISO is often the deal-breaker, not the champion.</li>
      <li><strong>Reference-forward selling.</strong> The fastest closes happen when the prospect's peer introduces them to your product. "A partner told me" outperforms any outbound sequence in this category.</li>
      <li><strong>Co-pilot framing fatigue.</strong> Buyers are skeptical of "AI co-pilot" messaging. The reps landing deals are talking about outcomes (time per contract, acceptance rate on suggested redlines) not features.</li>
    </ul>
