<h1>[Research] What are the recurring legal objections we've heard in enterprise deals?</h1>
<blockquote><p>What are the recurring legal objections we've heard in enterprise deals?</p></blockquote>
<h2>Summary</h2>
<p>Across the Keating &amp; Marsh deal and earlier enterprise conversations, three legal objections recur consistently: (1) incomplete DPA coverage, particularly for EU data residency and sub-processor disclosure; (2) lack of SOC 2 Type II certification; and (3) concerns about AI suggestion authority — who "signed off" on a playbook position and whether that carries legal defensibility.</p>
<h2>Key Findings</h2>
<h3>Keating deal surfaces the canonical enterprise objection pattern</h3>
<p>The Keating executive demo crystallized the objection pattern. During the demo, Amira Saleh's three DPA questions (sub-processor registry, data retention on termination, EU data residency timeline) represent exactly the objections that slow or kill enterprise legal-tech evaluations. Peter Nakagawa's closing question — "what's the one thing you want us to stop doing?" — also surfaced a deeper concern about whether Ellery's playbook positions would hold up under scrutiny by outside counsel.<sup><a href="seed:meetings/mtg-keating-exec-demo">1</a></sup></p>
<h3>Keating Deal Health post documents the live objection queue</h3>
<p>Tessa's Keating Deal Health post tracks the current open items that map directly to recurring legal objections: Arjun addressing SOC 2 questionnaire question #44, Maren drafting the DPA language, and the pricing revision tied to the matters-volume model. The pattern is consistent across deals — SOC 2 and DPA questions are the two most frequent legal gate-keepers.<sup><a href="seed:posts/post-keating-health">2</a></sup></p>
<h3>External deal conversations confirm the DPA-first pattern</h3>
<p>The Soma Iyer conversation at Lake Forest Trust and the Amira Saleh proposal walk-through both show that DPA and data handling are asked in the first or second meeting, not the fourth. The legal objection arrives before the commercial one. The implication for sales motion: DPA readiness needs to be pre-packaged, not assembled deal-by-deal.<sup><a href="seed:email_threads/thread-mateo-amira">3</a></sup></p>
<h3>Keating goal frames the broader pattern</h3>
<p>The Keating goal captures the structural reality: SOC 2 and EU data residency are the only remaining blockers on their side. Both are legal-compliance objections, not product objections. The deal is moving to paper despite the objections because Ellery addressed them proactively — which is the playbook for replicating this motion in future enterprise deals.<sup><a href="seed:goals/keating">4</a></sup></p>
