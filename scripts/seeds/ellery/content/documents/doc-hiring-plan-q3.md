# Series A Hiring Plan — Q3 Sequencing

## Context

We closed the Series A three weeks ago. The board approved a hiring envelope of 22 heads over the next 12 months. Q3 is the first tranche. The goal is not to hire as fast as possible — it is to sequence the hires so that each one unblocks the next wave.

We are hiring seven people in Q3. This doc is the contract.

## The Seven Roles (in order)

1. **Chief of Staff** — Darren (DRI). Urgent. Target close: end of week 3. Sourcing through Watershed Partners. Three candidates in final loops.
2. **SRE / Infra Engineer** — Priya (DRI). Target close: week 4. Needed to own on-call and scale the contract processing pipeline before Keating goes live.
3. **ML Engineer** — Priya (DRI). Target close: week 5. Jordan cannot own evals, retrieval, and fine-tuning alone past September.
4. **Founding CSM** — Tessa (DRI). Target close: week 6. Needs to be hired before Keating signs so onboarding is not a Mateo-does-everything situation.
5. **Integrations Engineer** — Priya (DRI). Target close: week 8. Enterprise deals will require connectors (iManage, NetDocuments, SSO providers).
6. **Product Marketer** — Tessa (DRI, with Leo). Target close: week 10. Needed for the Redline Co-pilot GA.
7. **Legal Content Analyst #2** — Maren (DRI, with Rhea). Target close: week 12. Rhea cannot own playbook coverage for the whole customer base alone.

## Envelope Usage

Seven hires at fully-loaded cost lands at approximately $1.8M annualized, leaving ~$3.1M in the envelope for Q4 and H1 next year. That is deliberately conservative — we want room for one or two unplanned hires (a designer, possibly a second AE) if the pipeline demands it.

## What Could Go Wrong

- **CoS slips past week 3.** Then Darren stays the bottleneck on board prep, OKRs, and the exec rhythm. This is the single highest-leverage hire of the quarter.
- **SRE and ML collide in calendar.** Priya cannot run two parallel loops at full intensity. We will stagger by a week and lean on Jordan for the ML loop.
- **Keating closes before CSM hired.** Mateo + Tessa will cover temporarily. We accept this risk.

## Review Cadence

Weekly review in the Hiring chat, every Friday. Full review in the exec sync Monday. Owners update pipeline counts in `doc-req-tracker` by end of day Thursday.
