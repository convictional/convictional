## Scope

- **Users:** 40 seats, Keating & Marsh commercial contracts practice
- **Matter types:** commercial contracts — MSAs, NDAs, SOWs, DPAs
- **Playbook scope:** all commercial contract playbooks currently maintained by the commercial contracts practice
- **Geography:** US users on US tenant; any EU-based users on EU tenant (region-scoped storage, EU inference)
- **Integration:** iManage (document import), Okta (SSO)

## Out of Scope for Pilot

- M&A practice (targeted for expansion in month 10)
- London office (EU tenant exists for compliance; London users added in expansion)
- Outside-counsel review workflows
- Custom model fine-tuning on firm data (available in enterprise tier only)

## Term

18 months, effective on the later of (a) signature or (b) tenant provisioning complete. Renewal by mutual agreement at month 16 or earlier.

## Success Criteria (Mutual)

By month 9, we and Keating review the pilot against:
- Average redline cycle time for commercial contracts (target: 30%+ reduction)
- Associate-hours per matter (target: 25%+ reduction on first-pass redlines)
- Ground truth accuracy on Keating playbooks (target: 95%+ agreement with senior-reviewed baseline)
- Associate satisfaction (target: NPS 40+)

If three of four hit, we move forward with expansion. If fewer than three hit, we have an honest conversation about why.
