# Interview Debrief — Priyanka Deol

On-site Thursday. Same panel as Naomi Tuesday. Plus the exercise.

## Summary Recommendation

**Strong yes.** The clearest signal we've had in this loop. Matching or beating Naomi on every dimension except pure operating rigor, and well ahead on judgment and trust-building. I would extend an offer.

## Panel Scores

- Darren (strategic judgment): 4 — "the strongest strategic judgment I've seen in this loop." Darren's words.
- Maren (decision-making): 4 — held her ground on the hard question about when a CoS shouldn't take ownership. Gave an answer I actually agreed with: "when ownership would substitute my judgment for a leader who needs to build their own muscle."
- Priya (operating rigor): 3 — solid but not dazzling. Her artifact examples were good; she's clearly shipped. Not as process-forward as Naomi.
- Tessa (collaboration): 4 — "she made me want to work together."

## The Exercise

Radically different shape from Naomi. She opened with: "I don't know the product well enough to say what you should do first. Here's what I'd spend the first two weeks learning, and here's what I'd expect to be able to tell you at the end of that period." Then she walked through a learning plan — who she'd shadow, what customer calls she'd sit in, what she'd read. Then she said "these are the three things I'd be paying attention to as signals that you need me to move faster."

That is the answer. Darren's face lit up.

## Where The Judgment Comes From

She was CoS at a Series B infrastructure company that hit scale problems at 60 people. She talked about the experience of realizing the CoS role had to shift from "executor" to "multiplier" around month four, and how she got there too slowly the first time. That self-awareness is exactly what I want in a CoS at a 12-person company about to scale.

## What Could Go Wrong

She is not the most operationally rigorous candidate. If we hire her, we are betting on judgment over process and expecting the process to follow. At 12 people that is right. At 40 people it might need an operations partner. We should be clear about that bet with her, not after.

## Reference Calls

I've lined up three reference calls for Monday. Her former CEO (we know him through the Watershed network), a former peer, and one of her former ICs. I'll write up the reference take by Tuesday.

## Recommendation

Offer. Target close: end of next week.
