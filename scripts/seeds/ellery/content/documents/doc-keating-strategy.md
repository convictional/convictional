# Keating & Marsh — Deal Strategy

This is the single biggest deal in our pipeline and the deal that gates whether we can repeat the motion in our segment. Writing it down so the team executes against the same map.

## The Account

Keating & Marsh is an AmLaw 75 full-service firm. ~900 lawyers, ~$780M revenue. Strong M&A and commercial contracts practice. HQ in Chicago, offices in London, Singapore, Frankfurt. EU presence is the reason data residency is on the table.

They have tried and failed to deploy two AI contract tools in the past four years. They are skeptical by default, specifically about hallucinations and about tools that don't respect firm playbooks. They are also under intense pressure from their top clients to show AI adoption.

## Buying Committee

- **Amira Chen** — Partner, Innovation Committee chair. Champion. She brought us in. Believes in us.
- **David Markham** — Managing Partner. Economic buyer. Signs anything over $500K. Risk-averse, skeptical of vendors, but trusts Amira.
- **Jennifer Ozawa** — General Counsel (yes, GCs have GCs). Security and compliance review. She will block us if SOC 2 and EU residency aren't real.
- **Ryan Tomlin** — Head of Practice Innovation. Operational owner if we win. Will run the pilot.
- **Darius Haddad** — CISO. SOC 2 Type I is non-negotiable. Already flagged concerns about data residency.

## Competitive Landscape

Two competitors in the room:
1. **Harvey** — brand-name, deeper adoption curve with BigLaw. Weakness: playbook coverage is shallow for commercial contracts, weaker on redline explainability.
2. **An internal build** — Keating has ~6 engineers prototyping an internal tool. Weakness: they will not ship in 2026.

Our wedge: playbook-aware redlines, ground-truth quality, EU data residency, and a co-pilot UX that mirrors how their senior associates actually work. We demo better than anyone in this segment.

## The Thesis

**Land:** 18-month pilot, 40 seats in commercial contracts team, $420K/year. We do not try to sell the whole firm. We win the practice that has the cleanest metric (deal cycle time) and the champion we already have (Amira).

**Expand:** If the pilot metrics are good at month 9, we expand to M&A practice (160 seats) and then to London (another 120 seats). The expansion path is roughly 4-5x the land ACV within 18 months.

## Pricing

- Pilot: $420K/year, 40 seats. No discounting below $10K/seat.
- Expansion tier at $8.5K/seat starting at 100 seats.
- EU residency tier: $2K/seat uplift. This is a real cost, not a rent-seeking line. We pass SOC 2 Type II costs into the EU tier.

## Close Plan

- **Week 1:** SOC 2 Type I audit kickoff, EU residency ADR published, pricing aligned
- **Week 3:** exec demo for Amira, David, Ryan
- **Week 5:** Jennifer + Darius security review
- **Week 6-7:** proposal iteration with Amira
- **Week 9:** target countersign

## Risks

- **Darius blocks on security.** This is the #1 risk. SOC 2 Type I is the floor, not the ceiling. We need to be prepared for a real pentest ask.
- **David takes it to finance for alternatives.** Hard to defend if Harvey comes in 20% cheaper. Our defense is Amira's voice and the playbook depth.
- **Pilot scope creeps.** Maren and I have a rule: no more than one scope-addition during negotiation. Everything else is post-countersign.
