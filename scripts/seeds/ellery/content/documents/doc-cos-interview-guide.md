# CoS Interview Guide

Goal: a consistent, rigorous loop so the panel produces comparable signal across candidates. Everyone on the panel should read this and the scorecard before their session.

## Loop Shape

- **30-min recruiter screen** (Watershed) — values fit, compensation, logistics
- **60-min conversation with Darren** — strategic judgment, past CoS or CoS-adjacent work
- **On-site (4 hours)** — four 45-min panels + 60-min exercise + 30-min wrap
- **Debrief same day**, decision within 48 hours

## On-Site Panels

### Panel 1 — Darren (45 min) — Strategic judgment
Opens the on-site. Tone-setter. Questions:
- "Walk me through a time you disagreed with your principal. How did it resolve?"
- "What is the hardest operating decision you've helped a leader make?"
- "What does a CoS do that a really good COO doesn't?"

### Panel 2 — Maren (45 min) — Decision-making and judgment
- "Describe a decision you owned. How did you decide who had to be in the room?"
- "Tell me about a time you held a hard line against your principal."
- "When should a CoS NOT take ownership of something?"

### Panel 3 — Priya or Leo (45 min) — Operating rigor
- "Walk me through an operating rhythm you designed. What worked? What didn't?"
- "Show me a memo or artifact you're proud of."
- "How do you handle a team member who is underperforming but has a close relationship with the CEO?"

### Panel 4 — Tessa (45 min) — Collaboration and trust-building
- "Tell me about a time you won over a skeptic inside your own company."
- "What's your read on whether a team has good trust? How do you build it?"
- "How do you handle the 'CoS as spy' perception?"

## The Exercise (60 min)

"What Would You Do First" — we give the candidate a one-pager about Ellery's current state (Series A closed, 5 threads, bottleneck on Darren). They have 30 minutes to prep, 20 minutes to present their first-30-days plan, 10 minutes Q&A.

We are not looking for the "right" plan. We are looking for:
- How they prioritize
- How they ask clarifying questions
- How they handle "I don't know" — do they make something up or do they say so?
- Whether their instincts match ours

## Debrief

Same day, one hour after the candidate leaves. Each panelist scores 1-4 on the scorecard dimensions and writes two sentences per dimension. No panelist speaks before writing. We want independent signal.

## Bar

Strong hire = at least three "4" scores across competencies, zero counter-indicators. "Maybe" = no. We do not hire "maybe" for this role.
