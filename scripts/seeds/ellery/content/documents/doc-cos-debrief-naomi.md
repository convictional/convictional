# Interview Debrief — Naomi Fisk

On-site Tuesday. Full panel: me, Maren, Priya, Tessa. Plus the exercise.

## Summary Recommendation

**Lean no.** Strong candidate on paper, strong in rooms individually, but the exercise exposed a judgment mismatch that I can't get comfortable with. I'd rather keep looking than hire a maybe.

## Panel Scores

- Darren (strategic judgment): 3 — sharp, articulate. Past CoS role at a Series C fintech. Some rough edges when pushed.
- Maren (decision-making): 2 — didn't love the answer on "when should a CoS NOT take ownership." Felt evasive.
- Priya (operating rigor): 3 — solid artifacts. Walked us through a quarterly planning process she ran. Genuinely impressive.
- Tessa (collaboration): 4 — the highest score of the day. Tessa said "I'd want her in the foxhole with me." Believable.

## The Exercise

This is where it got interesting. Her "first 30 days" plan was almost entirely about installing process — exec retros, OKR framework, board cadence. No mention of meeting people, no mention of understanding the product, no mention of learning the customer.

When I pushed — "what would you do if Darren said 'don't install the framework yet, learn first'?" — she said "I'd push back. I think the framework unlocks the learning." That is a fine answer from a McKinsey consultant. It is not the right answer from the person I want running my exec rhythm.

## What's Pulling Me Back To "Lean No"

The role needs someone who can hold the paradox: they need to ship operating artifacts AND they need to earn trust with execs who are already running hot. If the first 30 days feels like "Naomi is installing process on me," we lose half the leverage of the hire. I don't think she reads that risk.

Maren agrees. Tessa is the strongest "yes" voice — she weighted the collaboration signal heavily. Priya is neutral.

## Where I Could Be Wrong

She's the most operationally rigorous candidate we've seen. If we believe the top risk is "nobody ships the artifact," she solves that risk. If we believe the top risk is "exec trust erodes," she might deepen it.

## Next Step

See how Priyanka does Thursday. If Priyanka is at-or-above Naomi on scorecard, easy call. If Priyanka is below, we reopen this conversation.
