# Chief of Staff Scorecard

The scorecard is the contract between me and the person we hire. If they deliver on this, the hire works. If they don't, the hire doesn't, and we should know by 90 days.

## Mission

Be the force multiplier for Darren and the exec team. Own the operating rhythm. Translate strategy into execution. Be the person everyone trusts to close the loop.

## Outcomes

### First 30 days
- Has shadowed every exec 1:1 and understands each leader's current top-three priorities
- Has taken ownership of the weekly exec sync agenda and notes
- Has a draft of the Q3 operating rhythm (weekly/monthly/quarterly cadence) ready for exec review
- Has met every full-time employee at least once

### First 60 days
- Owns the board prep process end-to-end, including the first draft of the board deck
- Has shipped the exec operating rhythm and it is running without Darren needing to chase
- Runs the OKR drafting process for Q4
- Has built a "what Darren owns" vs "what CoS owns" split that both of us can point to

### First 90 days
- Is the DRI for the board meeting. Darren presents; CoS owns the artifact and the follow-ups.
- Has absorbed at least two ongoing projects that are currently on Darren's plate (candidates: exec recruiting ops, investor updates, company-wide comms)
- Team survey shows the exec team feels more in-sync than 90 days prior

## Competencies

- **Strategic judgment** — Can hold nuance. Can say "no" on Darren's behalf without being a jerk about it.
- **Operating rigor** — Ships artifacts. Follows up. Closes loops. Never drops a ball.
- **Written communication** — Writes clearly. Edits ruthlessly. Can turn a messy meeting into a clean memo.
- **Trust-building** — Earns trust fast with execs, ICs, and the board.
- **Executive presence** — Can hold the room. Can speak truth to power (me) without flinching.

## Counter-Indicators

- "CoS as calendar manager." No. We have scheduling infrastructure for that.
- "CoS as ghost-writer who never steps out front." No. This person owns artifacts with their name on them.
- "CoS as chief-of-nothing who just runs meetings." No. They are DRI on outcomes.
- Wants this job as a stepping stone to be CEO. Red flag. We want someone who wants this job for what it is.
