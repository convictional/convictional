# Keating Exec Demo Script

**When:** Week 3, Thursday, 11am CT, 45 minutes
**Who's in the room:** Amira (champion), David (MP), Ryan (innovation lead). No Darius or Jennifer this round.
**Who's presenting:** Darren opens (5 min), Maren does the legal narrative (8 min), I drive the demo (20 min), Darren closes (5 min), Q&A (7 min)

## The Arc

We are not selling software. We are telling them how their best senior associate spends two more hours a day on partner-track work and two fewer hours a day on redlines that a co-pilot could have done first pass. Everything in the demo laddered to that story.

## The Magic Moment

At the 12-minute mark of the demo I upload a real-ish Keating-style MSA redline and the co-pilot shows its proposed markup with **every** change citing the specific playbook provision. Amira has already told me this is the feature her partners want to see. The room goes quiet, she smiles, David leans forward. That is the moment.

If the magic moment doesn't land, we have lost the demo. Everything after is recovery.

## Structure

### Darren — 5 min
- Brief company intro. Ellery, Series A, lawyers love the product. No pitch deck. One slide, one line: "We build the AI contract co-pilot that respects your playbook."
- Acknowledge their past experience: "We know you've looked at two AI tools before. We know what went wrong. We built differently."

### Maren — 8 min
- The legal narrative. Not a demo; a story. The shape of the redline problem from a lawyer's chair.
- "Why we think the current generation of tools fails you: they hallucinate, they ignore playbooks, they give partners work to check instead of work to approve."
- Lead into the demo with the question: "What would it look like if the co-pilot knew your playbook better than your newest associate?"

### Mateo — 20 min (the demo)
1. Upload a Keating-style MSA (I have a sanitized version Amira helped me tune)
2. Show the co-pilot's pass: every clause tagged against their playbook
3. **The magic moment:** the redline view, with citations
4. Show the lawyer-in-the-loop flow — accept, reject, revise
5. Show the ground-truth audit: "Here is every time the co-pilot has been wrong in the last 100 redlines and why."
6. Show data residency: EU tenant, EU storage, EU inference. Show the audit log.

### Darren — 5 min close
- The commercial ask: 40 seats, 18-month pilot, commercial contracts team
- The partnership frame: "This is our flagship AmLaw account. We will treat it like it."
- No price discussion in the room. That goes to David via Amira after.

### Q&A — 7 min
Expected questions and our rehearsed answers:
- "What about hallucinations?" — Maren. Ground truth metric, published audit.
- "What about our playbook IP?" — Priya (on video). Playbooks remain firm property, never trained on.
- "How long to set up?" — me. Six-week pilot ramp, three-week playbook ingestion.
